Complex Plane: f(t) = c₀ + c₁e^{jω₀t} + c₋₁e^{-jω₀t} + c₂e^{j2ω₀t} + c₋₂e^{-j2ω₀t}

What you are seeing:

  • The Complex Plane (Left): The Real axis points UP, and the Imaginary axis points RIGHT.
  • Conjugate Pairs: You can see vectors rotating in opposite directions. Because $f(t)$ is a real signal, the side-to-side (imaginary) movements constantly cancel each other out.
  • The Final Tip: Notice how the very last point only moves straight up and down on the Real axis. It never strays into the imaginary space!
